# Overworld, product one-pager

**What it is:** Map Habitica tasks by zone and archive what it deletes

**Who it is for:** A person who already runs their life in Habitica, uses the
phone widget to capture, and has hit the two walls the app does not admit to: it
cannot tell them what they finished last quarter, and it cannot tell them what
they could get done while they are already out of the house.

**Register:** product (a tool someone uses), not brand (a page someone visits).

**The scene:** Standing in a doorway with keys in hand, phone or laptop open for
under a minute, deciding whether this trip out can absorb two more errands.
Secondarily, ten minutes on a Sunday evening looking at what the week actually
produced. Both are short, both are impatient, neither wants a landing page.

That scene sets the rest: dark by default because it is opened at the end of the
day as often as the start; dense enough that a zone's whole mission list fits
without scrolling; and the primary board answers its question in one glance with
no interaction beyond picking a zone.

**What done looks like:** The session ends with the person knowing what to do
next in the place they are standing, having captured anything new in one line,
and with today's completions safely archived locally so the 30-day deletion
cannot take them.
